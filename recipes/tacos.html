Mexican Tacos

Chicken Tacos Description

Chicken tacos are soft or crispy tortillas filled with seasoned grilled chicken,
fresh veggies, and zesty sauces for a bright, flavorful bite.

How to Prepare Chicken Tacos (Step-by-Step)

1. Prepare the Chicken Filling

  • Heat oil in a pan on medium flame.
  • Add chopped onions and sauté until soft.
  • Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  • Add shredded or small-cut chicken pieces.
  • Season with chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  • Add a splash of water or tomato sauce and cook until chicken absorbs the flavor.
  • Stir well and turn off the heat.

2. Warm the Tortillas

  • Heat a pan (no oil needed).
  • Warm each tortilla for 10–15 seconds per side.
  • Keep them soft and warm in a cloth or foil.

3. Prepare the Toppings

  • Shred lettuce.
  • Chop tomatoes.
  • Slice onions thinly.
  • Grate cheese (cheddar or mozzarella).
  • Prepare salsa or keep store-bought ready.
  • Cut lime slices.

4. Assemble the Tacos

  • Take a warm tortilla.
  • Add a spoon of chicken filling.
  • Add lettuce, tomatoes, and onions.
  • Sprinkle cheese on top.
  • Add salsa or hot sauce if you like spicy.
  • Squeeze a little lime juice.

5. Serve

  • Serve immediately while warm.
  • Pair with nachos or sauces.
  • Enjoy your fresh chicken tacos!
