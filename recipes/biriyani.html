Ingredients

For The Rice

  • 2 cups Basmati rice
  • 3–4 cups water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 cardamom
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • Salt (to taste)

For The Chicken Masala

  • 500g chicken (cleaned and cut)
  • 2 large onions (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 green chilies (slit)
  • 2 tbsp ginger–garlic paste
  • ½ cup curd (yogurt)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp red chili powder
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint leaves
  • ½ cup coriander leaves
  • 4 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt (as needed)

For Final Dum

  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Saffron water / turmeric water (optional)
  • Fried onions (optional)

How to Prepare (Step-by-Step)

1. Cook the Rice

  • Wash basmati rice and soak for 20 minutes.
  • Boil water with bay leaves, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, and salt.
  • Add rice and cook until 70% done (grain should break with slight pressure).
  • Drain and keep aside.

2. Prepare the Chicken Masala

  • Heat oil/ghee in a large pot.
  • Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  • Add ginger–garlic paste and cook until raw smell goes.
  • Add tomatoes and green chilies and cook until soft.
  • Add turmeric, red chili powder, biryani masala, and garam masala and mix well.
  • Add chicken pieces and salt and cook for 10–12 minutes.
  • Add curd, mint, and coriander and mix well.
  • Cook until chicken is 80% done and masala thickens.

3. Layering (Dum Process)

  • Reduce flame to low.
  • Layer chicken masala at the bottom.
  • Add cooked rice on top.
  • Sprinkle mint and coriander leaves.
  • Drizzle ghee and saffron/turmeric water.
  • Close lid tightly (or seal with chapati dough).
  • Cook on low flame for 15–20 minutes.

4. Serve

  • Gently mix layers without breaking the rice.
  • Serve hot with onion raita, boiled egg, or salna/gravy.
