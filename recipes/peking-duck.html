Chinese Peking Duck

Chinese Peking Duck Description

Peking Duck is a classic Chinese dish known for its crispy golden skin and tender meat, served with thin pancakes, hoisin sauce, cucumber, and spring onions. Each wrap combines sweetness, crunch, and rich roasted flavors for a perfect, elegant bite.

How to Prepare Peking Duck (Step-by-Step)

1. Prepare the Duck

  • Clean the duck thoroughly and pat it dry.
  • Remove any leftover feathers and trim excess fat.
  • Prick the skin all over with a sharp fork (avoid piercing the meat).
  • Pour boiling water over the duck to tighten the skin.
  • Pat dry again and place the duck on a rack.

2. Make the Glaze

  • Mix soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, and a little five-spice powder.
  • Brush this glaze all over the duck evenly.
  • Hang or keep the duck uncovered in the fridge for 12–24 hours to dry the skin.

3. Roast the Duck

  • Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  • Place the duck on a rack, breast-side up.
  • Roast for 1 hour.
  • Flip the duck and roast for 30 more minutes.
  • Flip again (breast up) and roast another 10–15 minutes to crisp the skin.

4. Prepare the Wrappers & Sides

  • Steam thin Mandarin pancakes (store-bought or homemade).
  • Slice cucumber into thin strips.
  • Slice spring onions thinly.
  • Keep hoisin sauce ready.

5. Carve the Duck

  • Use a sharp knife to slice the crispy skin first.
  • Then slice the tender meat underneath.
  • Keep skin and meat separate (traditional style).

6. Serve

  • Place a piece of crispy skin and duck meat on a pancake.
  • Add cucumber and spring onion.
  • Drizzle hoisin sauce.
  • Roll it up and enjoy!
